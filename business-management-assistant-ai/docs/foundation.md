# Clean-slate product foundation

> Status (0.3): implemented are authenticated roles, policy-gated approvals, a hash-chained audit trail, record management, and an LLM-planned assistant. Not yet built: documents/knowledge base, live connectors, scheduler, multi-tenancy. The sections below describe the target blueprint.

This is a new business project with no inherited projects, memories, or assumptions. The assistant is intended to coordinate strategy, operations, projects, finance operations, contracts, customers, marketing, knowledge, and automation through an auditable orchestrator.

## Operating principles

1. Fresh start: import nothing without explicit approval.
2. One source of truth: link goals, work, people, documents, money, and decisions.
3. Action over conversation: convert requests into plans, tasks, drafts, and outcomes.
4. Least privilege: grant each connector only the scopes it needs.
5. Human approval for irreversible, regulated, public, financial, legal, or security-sensitive actions.
6. Evidence-first: keep sources and confidence with important claims.
7. Observable autonomy: record who proposed, approved, executed, and changed every action.
8. Modular growth: add specialist agents and adapters without replacing the core.

## Planned specialist agents

Chief of Staff, Planner, Operations, Research, Finance Operations, Commercial, Contracts, Marketing, Knowledge, Integration, Quality/Risk, and Learning.

## First 90-day outcomes

The next product phase should establish business identity and jurisdiction, define the product and customer, set goals, configure permissions, connect approved integrations, and launch the first ten repeatable workflows. Until those choices are supplied, the application remains in planning/sandbox mode.
